# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep Fishies players safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue for a security problem.**

Email <f@fishies.lol> with:

- what you found and where (URL, endpoint, or in-game action),
- the steps to reproduce it,
- what an attacker could do with it.

You will get a reply within 72 hours. Please give us a reasonable window to ship a fix
before disclosing anything publicly.

## In scope

- `fishies.lol` and its subdomains
- the Fishies desktop and mobile applications
- Fishies accounts, tanks, and social features

## Out of scope

- reports produced only by automated scanners, with no working proof of concept
- denial of service, volumetric or brute-force testing against production
- social engineering of players or staff
- missing best-practice headers with no demonstrated impact

We do not run a paid bounty programme yet, but we credit every valid report that asks
to be credited.

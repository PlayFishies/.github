---

Raise fish, decorate your tank, and visit anyone's aquarium at `fishies.lol/@username`.
Fish keep growing while you are away, react to every food pellet, and your tank stays
alive whether you are watching or not.

Fishies runs in the browser and is coming to desktop, mobile and Steam.

### About this organization

Fishies itself is **closed source** — the game's code lives in a private repository.
This organization is the public front door: feedback, issue tracking, press material and
anything we do open up lives here.

# Code of conduct

Fishies is a calm place. The same goes for everything in this organization.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Keep reports and comments on the subject: the game, the bug, the idea.
- Respect a "no" — not every request fits the game we are building.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks
- Sexual content, or anything targeting minors
- Posting anyone's private information
- Spam, advertising, or scam links
- Cheating tools, exploits shared publicly, or instructions for either

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or delete any contribution, and may block accounts,
without notice. Report anything that needs attention to <f@fishies.lol>.
